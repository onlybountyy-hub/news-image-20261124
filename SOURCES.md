1. **HERO — Russian drone strays into Poland.** A Russian unmanned aerial

   missiles had landed in Poland; NATO is deploying an AWACS early-warning
   aircraft to Poland in response.

   airspace, Polish armed forces say" (24 Nov 2026).
   https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/russian-unmanned-aerial-vehicle-briefly-violated-polish-airspace-polish-armed-2026-11-23/

2. **South Korea — Seoul sounds the alarm.** South Korea briefly issued an
   evacuation warning on Tuesday for Ulleung island off the east coast after
   a Russian aircraft entered the area; the warning was issued at 7:52 a.m.
   local time and lifted minutes later. The military scrambled fighter jets
   after multiple Russian planes entered the air defence identification zone;
   it said there was no intrusion of South Korean airspace.
   Source: Reuters, "South Korea briefly issues evacuation warning after
   Russia aircraft enters airspace" (24 Nov 2026).
   https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-korea-briefly-issues-evacuation-warning-after-russia-aircraft-enters-2026-11-23/

3. **Chess — crown on the line in Geneva.** The 2026 FIDE World Championship
   between defending champion Gukesh Dommaraju and challenger Javokhir
   Sindarov begins on Tuesday 24 November at 15:00 CET in Geneva; Sindarov
   has White in Game 1. Both players are 20 — the youngest world title
   match in history — over a maximum of 14 classical games.
   Source: Chess.com, "How To Watch FIDE World Championship 2026 Game 1"
   (24 Nov 2026).
   https://www.chess.com/news/view/2026-fide-world-chess-championship-game-1

   Source: EarthSky, "Visible planets and night sky guide for November"

   https://earthsky.org/astronomy-essentials/visible-planets-tonight-mars-jupiter-venus-saturn-mercury/
